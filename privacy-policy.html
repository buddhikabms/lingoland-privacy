Privacy Policy — LingoLand English

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy is written in English.

Made for children

LingoLand English is made for children aged 3 to 8. We do not collect any personal information from children — or from anyone else.

We collect nothing

We collect nothing. The app stores your child's progress — stars, levels, badges, traced letters, fish caught and games won — only on your own device. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, and no data is sent anywhere.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

There are no ads and no analytics in this version. The app does not track your child, does not know who they are, and cannot see their progress.

How the app works

All games, pictures, voices and songs are bundled with the app, and the app works fully offline. Playing a game makes no network request.

Your data, your control

You can delete all progress from Settings (Start Over). Deleting removes it from the device permanently. There is no copy anywhere else to delete, because we never had one.

Contact

If you contact support, only what you choose to send is shared. You can reach us at bmscharmed@gmail.com.
